02 / Featured · 2026

Probabilistic and approximate universal quantum purification machines

Quantum states and noisy processes can be represented as part of a larger, fully coherent system. This paper asks whether we can construct that larger description when the state or device is unknown and available only a limited number of times. It proves that finitely many copies cannot universally produce an exact purification, even if the procedure is allowed to succeed only sometimes. For approximation, it compares physical strategies by their average error and shows that different approaches are favoured depending on the size of the added quantum environment.

With Jessica Bavaresco
